    • GYM MUSIC SUCKS

    Tired of listening to the same boring songs every time you walk into the gym? It's time to change the playlist. Give gym members a voice in the music they hear while working out!

    What Is This Website?

    Gym Music Sucks is a website designed to give gym members more control over the music played in their workout environment.

    Everyone has different musical preferences. Some people enjoy metalcore and rock, while others prefer rap or electronic music. However, gym members usually have little say in what plays through the speakers.

    This website gives people an opportunity to recommend songs, vote for their favorites, and share their opinions about the current gym playlist.

    Why Does Gym Music Matter?

    Music is an important part of many people's workout experience. The right playlist can make exercising more enjoyable and help create a motivating atmosphere.

    Unfortunately, not everyone enjoys the music selected by their gym. Repetitive playlists and songs that don't match people's preferences can make the experience less enjoyable.

    Giving members a chance to recommend music could help gyms create playlists that better represent their communities.

    Our Goal

    The goal of this project is simple: make gym music better by giving the people listening to it a voice.

    Instead of allowing one person to decide what everyone hears, gym members can work together to recommend and vote on music.

    Whether you enjoy metalcore, rock, rap, electronic music, or something completely different, your recommendations matter.
